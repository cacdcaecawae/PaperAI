# Agent Note: PaperAI iOS visual language across the DSH shell and the product surfaces

Status: implemented

English | [中文](2026-10-19-paperai-ios-visual-language.zh.md)

## Problem

The [workbench visual grammar](2026-09-15-paperai-workbench-visual-grammar.md) gave PaperAI an ink-and-gold palette and a quiet control vocabulary, but only inside the product's own packages: the sidebar, the composer, menus, dialogs, and the settings panel kept the DSH geometry and its monochrome palette under a warm recolor. The user asked for a wholesale redesign in the iOS style and chose two things explicitly: system blue as the tint instead of gold, and a global change that includes the DSH core packages rather than only the token layer and the PaperAI packages.

## Decision

**Palette.** `ui-paperai-brand/src/client/theme.ts` remains the only place literal colors live, and it now carries the iOS system palette for both schemes. Light puts white content over the `#f2f2f7` grouped ground; dark puts black content over `#1c1c1e` grounds with `#2c2c2e` elevated cards, which is why `--dsw-alias-bg-layer-1` is `#2c2c2e` in dark while `--dsw-alias-bg-layer-2` is `#1c1c1e`: layer 2 is the ground (the desk, the settings panel, the inspector) and layers 1 and 3 are the cards on it. Labels are the iOS increased-contrast grays: secondary `#545458` / `#c7c7cc`, tertiary and caption `#636366` / `#aeaeb2`. Hover, press, and field fills are the translucent `rgba(118, 118, 128, …)` iOS fills. System blue is the brand accent, the selection, the focus ring, links, and the one filled button; status colors are the iOS red, green, and orange, deepened where they color type. Every text token holds WCAG AA 4.5:1, and the brand test computes it from the token values: on each solid ground (the base, the three layers, the sidebar), on the translucent gray fills of badges, hovered rows, and pop-up triggers painted over those grounds, and on each status or document type's own tint. Each scheme splits the tint. Fills under white type take `#0066cc` and `#0071e3` (5.6:1 and 4.7:1; `#007aff` holds 4.0:1 on white and white on the iOS `#0a84ff` 3.65:1), with a hover that darkens. Type and marks take `#0060c0` and `#64b0ff`, which still clear 4.5:1 on a tinted badge or a gray trigger on a card. Error type takes `#c80013` and `#ff8a82`, so it also clears a danger button's hover fill; light success and warning type take `#1b7031` and `#b83000`, since the iOS `#34c759` and `#ff9500` hold about 2:1 as type; and the document-type accents deepen in light and lift in dark until their badge type clears its own tint. Quaternary and dimmed labels stay low as disabled and placeholder ink, which the minimum exempts. Type on a filled control reads `--dsw-alias-button-primary-foreground` and the bubble's type `--dsw-specific-bubble-foreground`. The layer leaves `--dsw-alias-label-primary-foreground` alone: DSH reads it as the primary label's inverse (a checked checkbox's mark, the connection banner's type), and forcing it white turned that mark white on white in dark. Gold survives as `--paperai-brand-gold`, which the mark wears on both schemes, and, deepened, as the manuscript type accent. The user's sent message is an iMessage bubble: tint fill, white type, the bottom-right corner tightened to 6 px. The layer also sets the SF Pro font stack ahead of the platform UI faces and softens the shared `--dsw-shadow-lv1` to `lv3` elevations.

**Shared control aliases.** `ui-theme` declares seven aliases with DSH-neutral defaults so core primitives restyle through tokens rather than reading a product name: `--dsw-alias-focus-ring` (the tint at half strength, drawn outside a control as a 3 px ring instead of a 2 px outline), `--dsw-alias-material-bar` and `--dsw-alias-material-thick` (translucent chrome over a `blur(…) saturate(180%)` backdrop), `--dsw-alias-segment-fill` and `--dsw-alias-segment-track` with `--dsw-shadow-segment` for segmented controls, and `--dsw-alias-button-primary-foreground` and `--dsw-specific-bubble-foreground`, the type on the primary fill and in the user's bubble, which default to the label colors DSH already paired with those fills so the default web bundle reads as before. The track is its own alias rather than `--dsw-alias-bg-module-platform` because the scrollbar contract treats every `--dsw-alias-bg-*` value as an elevated surface. Because forced colors (Windows high contrast) drop box-shadow, controls that draw their focus ring over `outline: none` need a system-color fallback: `base.css` restores a `Highlight` outline on `:focus-visible` under `forced-colors: active`.

**Controls.** Buttons are capsules; the filled variant is the tint in semibold, the outline variant becomes the iOS gray button (translucent fill, tint text), and a press dims to 70 % opacity. Inputs are gray-filled r10 fields with the focus ring. Menus are r14 context menus on the thick material over a 40 px blur, with 6 px group bands for separators and a tint check mark. Dialogs keep r24 on the grouped ground, take a 17 px semibold title, and close through a gray-filled circle. Menus grow out of the anchor's corner (scale 0.94 to 1 over 180 ms), and dialogs and the settings panel fade in over 280 ms. The dialog entrance carries no transform: a portaled menu places itself from its trigger's on-screen rect and re-places only on scroll or resize, so a menu opened while the dialog still rose stayed offset after it settled. The menu's own scale is safe because placement reads `offsetWidth` and `offsetHeight`, which a transform leaves alone; `prefers-reduced-motion` turns both motions off. The toast is a thick-material HUD capsule in the label ink. Every two- or three-way view switch is a segmented control: the details-view tabs, the conversation's 对话 / 轨迹 tabs, the plugin settings tabs, the appearance picker (which replaces its three tiles), and the versions panel's two readings, which became plain buttons carrying `aria-pressed` because `Pill` exposes no pressed state.

**Shell.** The sidebar follows the iPadOS sidebar: tint-colored toolbar glyphs, 17 px bold section heads, a gray search field, r10 rows, the new-session button as a tinted button, the current session in the tinted fill with tint type, and the open document's row as the filled selection with white type, icon, and badge. Document rows draw a solid tint outline with a 2px gap outside the row, so the indicator cannot disappear into the selected fill. The conversation header keeps one hairline and puts its tabs in a segmented control; the hero headline is a 28/34 bold large title and the hero's blurred backdrop ellipse is deleted. The settings panel reads as the iPadOS Settings app: a 28 px large title over the nav, a tint-filled selected cell, 22 px bold page titles, and rows on white grouped cards (`GeneralSection` became one card; the model, plugin, preset, ACP, and template cards dropped their outlines for layer-1 fills, with a tint ring on the current preset and template).

**PaperAI surfaces.** The start page shows the gold mark on a 64 px app-icon tile, the project name as a large title, the template as a tint link, and the documents as an inset grouped list: one r12 group on the grouped ground, a 28 px type-tinted glyph tile per row, hairlines inset past the glyph column, a disclosure chevron, and a tint 新建或导入文档 row. The workbench header's chips are tint capsules beside the filled 导出; the command row and the side-panel header sit in the bar material; floating pills are thick-material capsules; the side panel is an inspector of white cards on the grouped ground (the gate status with its findings, the current template, and the version timeline each sit in one); pop-up triggers are gray-filled with tint values. Ink drawn on the page stays scheme-invariant and moves from gold to `#0071e3` for rules and `#0058c6` for type.

**Ownership.** The DSH core edits (ui-primitives, ui-theme, ui-sidebar, ui-workspace, ui-conversation, ui-settings-general, ui-settings-models, ui-settings-plugins, ui-agent-preset, ui-permission-presets, ui-message-feedback, ui-goal, ui-user-questions, ui-directory-picker-browse, locale, session-log-export) are PaperAI changes inside shared packages, counted in the footprint the [product-profile note](../architecture/2026-08-28-paperai-product-profile.md) tracks; a DSH update re-applies them. This decision supersedes the [workbench visual grammar](2026-09-15-paperai-workbench-visual-grammar.md) for the palette, the gold filled action, radii, the floating recipe, and the focus treatment; its header, command row, page, and sidebar structure stand.

## Alternatives considered

**Keep gold as the tint.** First-party iOS apps such as Notes keep their own tint, and the mark is gold. The user chose system blue as the more recognisably iOS reading; gold stays on the mark and the manuscript badge.

**Change only the token layer and the PaperAI packages.** That keeps the DSH merge cost at zero, but the sidebar rows, the composer, menus, dialogs, and the settings panel would keep DSH shapes under iOS colors. The user chose the global change and accepted the merge cost.

**Raise every control to iOS touch metrics (44 pt rows, 17 pt body).** The product is a desktop web app beside a page; the sidebar and settings keep their 32 to 40 px rows and 14 px body, and only titles move to the iOS scale.

**Keep the iOS `#0a84ff` under white type in dark.** It is the system value, but white on it holds 3.65:1 and 2.83:1 on its lighter hover; the split keeps the bright blue for type, where it reads, and a deeper fill under white.

**Raise dialogs with a transform and re-place menus when the motion ends.** It keeps the rise, but a menu opened mid-entrance still sits offset for the rest of the motion; a fade has nothing to correct.

**Reuse `--dsw-alias-bg-module-platform` for segmented tracks.** It has the right value, but the scrollbar contract reads every `--dsw-alias-bg-*` value as an elevated surface and would oblige unrelated scroll containers to rebind their thumbs.

## Consequences

The shell, settings, and product surfaces share one vocabulary, and the palette, focus ring, materials, and segmented controls stay retunable from `theme.ts` without touching component sheets. The cost is a larger PaperAI footprint inside DSH core sheets, a dark ladder where layer 1 sits lighter than layer 2, and translucent menus that rely on `backdrop-filter`. The PaperAI browser goldens are aria snapshots and replay unchanged, so the visual result is verified by screenshots. The brand test computes AA contrast for every text token over its grounds, fills, and tints, and for the page inks on white paper; the assembled project-navigation test checks the selected document's keyboard-focus outline; the modal spec rejects a transform in the dialog and settings entrance keyframes, and a ui-theme style check holds the forced-colors outline. Meeting AA costs a heavier gray ladder than stock iOS, whose secondary label holds 3.4:1 on white, and status and badge hues darker than the system ones. Three DSH strings that are not disabled still read the dimmed label (a preset card's id, one input-trigger menu row, the workspace browser's empty detail), as DSH ships them.
